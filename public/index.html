How it works

From “who paid?” to settled, in three steps.

  1. 01

    Make a group

    A trip, a hostel or flat, or anything else. Add yourself in one tap, and friends from your other groups in another.

  2. 02

    Log what's spent

    One person paid or several. Split it equally, by exact amounts, by shares or by percent. Leave halfway and your draft waits for you.

  3. 03

    Settle up

    See who owes whom, and the fewest payments that make everyone even. Tap Paid once the money's sent.

Many at once

Paste the group chat. It does the rest.

Copy the messages from WhatsApp and paste them in. Every amount is read, totalled and shown in a preview before anything is added. Short names and small typos still find the right person.

Settle up

A tangle of IOUs, untied.

Five friends, one long weekend, and everyone paid for something. Vilarci E-Split nets it all out and shows only the fewest payments that make everyone even. Mark each one Paid as it happens, and undo it if you tapped too soon.

Privacy

Your money stays on your phone.

No sign-up needed, and no tracking. Your groups live in a small database on your device.

  • Saved on your device

    Groups, people and settings are stored on the phone, not on a server.

  • Works offline

    Add expenses on a mountain road or in a hostel basement. No signal needed.

  • Leaves only when you say

    Data leaves the phone only when you share a report or export a backup.
