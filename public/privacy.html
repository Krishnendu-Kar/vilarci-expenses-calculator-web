Privacy policy

Vilarci E-Split splits shared expenses for trips, hostels and flats. This page says what the app keeps, where, and how to delete it. Last updated 12 November 2026.

Without an account

Everything stays on your phone: your groups, expenses, payments, people, your name, UPI ids and settings. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account (optional)

  • Your email address and password, to sign in. The password is stored by our sign-in provider as a one-way hash; nobody can read it. Once your email is confirmed, people in your live groups can see it (see below).
  • Your name and up to 3 UPI ids, so they are kept safe and can be shown to people you split with.
  • This is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, in Mumbai, India.

Live groups (only when you put a group online or join one)

  • The group's name and type, its members' names, and the expenses, payments and monthly repeats you or others add, so everyone in the group sees the same numbers.
  • An Activity list of who changed what and when (for example "Neha edited Dinner"), so nothing changes without the group seeing it.
  • Only the group's members can see it. They also see your name and your UPI ids, so they know where to pay you, and your confirmed email address on your page in the app, so they know the account is really you. Nobody else can see your email: not people outside your live groups, not people who have left them, and there is no list or search of emails.
  • Groups that are not put online never leave your phone.
  • How long things are kept: deleted expenses and payments for up to 90 days, Activity for 180 days, and a group deleted for everyone for 30 days (so an admin can restore it). Then they are removed for good.

What we never do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.
  • Your fingerprint or face never reaches the app: when you lock a group, your phone checks it and only says yes or no.
  • Google: if you use "Continue with Google", Google tells us your email address and name to make your account. If you turn on Google Drive backup, your groups are saved in a hidden folder of your own Google Drive that only this app can read; we never receive that copy.
  • Contacts: only when you tap "From contacts" while adding people to a group, the app reads names from your phone's contacts to show them to you. Nothing is sent to us; only the names you pick are saved, as people in your groups (and, for a live group, shared with its members like any name you type).

What you share yourself

Reports (PDF or message) and backup files contain the names, amounts and UPI ids in them. They go only where you send them.

Deleting your data

  • Account: in the app, Settings → Account → Delete account. You leave every live group and your account, name and UPI ids are deleted right away. See also esplit.vilarci.in/delete-account.
  • Live groups: leave a group, or (admins) delete it for everyone. Your name stays on the group's old expenses, marked as left, because the others still need the numbers to add up.
  • On your phone: delete groups in the app, or uninstall it.
