Delete your Vilarci E-Split account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Vilarci E-Split and go to Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email [contact email] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password), your name and your UPI ids, from our servers.

What stays

  • Groups saved on your phone stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. They were never on our servers.
