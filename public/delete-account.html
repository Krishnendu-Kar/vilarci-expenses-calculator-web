Delete your Vilarci E-Split account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Vilarci E-Split and go to Profile, then the settings button (the gear) at the top.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

You leave every live group, and your account is deleted right away.

First, the app checks that you can leave each live group. If you still owe money or are owed in one, are in one of its monthly repeats, or are its only admin, it lists what to do first (for example "Goa trip: you owe ₹450 here. Settle up first."). A debt paid outside the app can be marked as paid by you, the other person, or a group admin.

Without the app

Email support@vilarci.in from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We check the same things as the app, delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done (or say what needs settling first).

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password), your name and your UPI ids, from our servers.
  • Your place in every live group (you leave them all).

What stays

  • Groups only on your phone stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. They were never on our servers.
  • In live groups, your name stays on old expenses and payments, marked as left, so the others' numbers still add up. Admins can delete a group for everyone; it is then removed for good after 30 days.
