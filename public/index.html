How it works

From “who paid?” to settled, in three steps.

  1. Make a group

    A trip, a hostel or flat, or anything else. Keep it on this phone, or put it online and send the link. An unfinished group waits as a draft.

  2. Log what's spent

    One person paid or several. Split equally, by amounts, shares or percent. Or paste the group chat and let it read every number.

  3. Settle up

    See who owes whom and the fewest payments that make everyone even. Pay by UPI right from there, then tap Paid.

Questions

Before you ask.

Anything else? Write to support@vilarci.in.

Is Vilarci E-Split free?

Yes. It's free on Google Play, with no ads and no tracking.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Groups you keep on your phone work without an account. You only sign in, with Google or email, to put a group online and share it with friends.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Add and edit expenses anywhere. Online groups catch up by themselves when you're back on the internet.

How do friends join my group?

Put the group online and share its invite link or QR code, or tell them the 8-letter code. They install the app, sign in and join. Admins can approve each new member first.

Can it handle hostel mess and flat expenses month by month?

Yes. Hostel and flat groups open on the current month, an expense can count for another month, and Members and Settle up stay all-time, so nothing is lost between months.

How does it work out who pays whom?

It nets out what everyone paid and owes, then shows the fewest payments that make everyone even. Amounts are kept to the paisa, so the parts always add up.

Can friends pay me by UPI?

Yes. Save your UPI id in your profile. In Settle up, Pay opens any UPI app with your id and the amount already filled in.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Bengali and Tamil. The whole app switches at once, even the reports you share.

Is it a good Splitwise alternative in India?

It's made for how groups in India split money: rupees and paise, UPI payments, hostel months, Indian languages, and amounts pasted straight from a WhatsApp chat. It works offline and has no ads.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Vilarci E-Split is on Google Play for Android phones, tablets and Chromebooks.

Privacy

Your money, your choice.

No sign-up needed, no ads and no tracking. Groups live on your phone until you choose to put one online.

  • Saved on your device

    Groups, people and settings are stored on the phone. A group goes online only when you put it online, and then only its members see it.

  • Works offline

    Add expenses on a mountain road or in a hostel basement. No signal needed.

  • Leaves only when you say

    Data leaves the phone only when you put a group online, share a report, or back up to your own Google Drive. Read the privacy policy.
